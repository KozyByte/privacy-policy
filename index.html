Regex Quest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Regex Quest is developed and published by KozyByte.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Regex Quest mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Regex Quest itself does not require you to create an account and does not operate its own user accounts, analytics system, crash reporting system, or backend server for collecting personal information.

The App stores certain game-related information locally on your device, such as game progress and settings. This information is not transmitted to Kozybyte.

However, the App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google may automatically collect and process certain information when advertisements are displayed or interacted with.

Depending on how Google's advertising services are used, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as:

  • IP address
  • Device and advertising identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID
  • App and advertising interaction information
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used for advertising, measuring advertising performance, fraud prevention, and related advertising services.

For more information about Google's data practices, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure

2. Local Storage

Regex Quest stores game progress and application settings locally on your device.

KozyByte does not receive or have access to this locally stored game data.

If you uninstall the App, locally stored App data may be removed by Android according to the device's settings and behavior.

3. Advertising

Regex Quest uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may process device and advertising-related information as described above. Advertising data may be used to provide, measure, and improve advertising services and to help prevent fraud and abuse.

The App currently uses test advertisements during development and testing. Production advertising may be enabled in future releases.

4. Data Security

Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is protected in transit using encryption according to Google's documentation.

KozyByte does not operate a backend service for Regex Quest and does not independently collect or store the information processed by Google Mobile Ads.

5. Data Retention

KozyByte does not maintain a separate database containing personal information collected through Regex Quest.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google's own privacy practices and retention policies.

For details, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy .

6. Children's Privacy

Regex Quest is intended for users aged 18 and older.

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13.
